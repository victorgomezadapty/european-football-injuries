# Handoff limitations

Read `docs/limitations.md` for the full limitations and `reports/milestone_01_summary.md` for the observed quality counts.

Use the results as recorded absence consequences. The most consequential unresolved issues are repeated same-interval records, provisional name identities, unknown endpoint meaning, source age/context timing and missing eligibility/exposure denominators. Candidate-collapsed results are sensitivity estimates; they do not adjudicate club attribution.

Do not use full-history profiles as historical onset predictors, call repeated labels clinical reinjury, calculate an availability percentage or interpret raw position/league counts as risk. Survival modeling and external-data integration require verified identities, clinical endpoints, observation windows and exposure inputs; they are not supported by the current source alone.
